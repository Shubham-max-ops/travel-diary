01 · Japan

Where tradition meets tomorrow.

Japan brings together peaceful gardens, centuries-old temples and energetic modern cities. Take time to notice the details: a quiet shrine, a neighbourhood market or the glow of lanterns after sunset.

  • Culture: Tea ceremony, calligraphy and seasonal festivals
  • Food: Sushi, ramen and matcha sweets
  • Experience: Explore Kyoto's historic streets and a traditional market
Explore local flavours →
02 · Italy

La dolce vita, every day.

Italy is a celebration of art, architecture, food and everyday life. Wander through historic piazzas, admire beautiful old buildings and make room for a long meal at a local trattoria.

  • Culture: Renaissance art, music and lively piazzas
  • Food: Pizza, pasta and gelato
  • Experience: Walk through Rome's historic centre or explore Venice's canals
See the travel gallery →
03 · Switzerland

Into the great outdoors.

Switzerland invites you to slow down and look around. Snow-covered peaks, green valleys and glassy lakes create a dramatic setting for scenic train rides and outdoor adventures.

  • Culture: Alpine traditions, local crafts and village festivals
  • Food: Cheese fondue, rösti and Swiss chocolate
  • Experience: Take a mountain walk and enjoy a lakeside village
